Save Convo Clip as a desktop app

Open Convo Clip from your Dock or taskbar, in its own window, like any other app. It still runs in your browser, so your videos stay on your computer.

Chrome or Edge

  1. Select the Install icon at the right end of the address bar.
    No icon? In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu, then Cast, save, and share > Install page as app. In Edge, open the … menu, then Apps > Install this site as an app.
  2. Select Install.
  3. To keep it in your Dock, Control-click the Convo Clip icon in the Dock, then choose Options > Keep in Dock.

Safari

  1. Choose File > Add to Dock. This needs macOS Sonoma or later.
  2. Select Add. Convo Clip appears in your Dock and in your Applications folder.

WebM export works best in Chrome or Edge.

Microsoft Edge

  1. Open the … menu, then Apps > Install this site as an app.
  2. Select Install.
  3. When asked, choose Pin to taskbar or Pin to Start, then select Allow.

Google Chrome

  1. Select the Install icon at the right end of the address bar.
    No icon? Open the ⋮ menu, then Cast, save, and share > Install page as app.
  2. Select Install.
  3. To pin it, right-click Convo Clip in the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar.

To remove it later, open Convo Clip, open the menu in its title bar, then choose Uninstall. In Safari, drag it out of the Dock and delete it from Applications.
